Explore Our Solar System

>Quick Facts

☀️ Age of the Solar System Our solar system formed approximately 4.6 billion years ago from a giant cloud of gas and dust. 🪐 Eight Planets There are eight planets in our solar system, each with its own unique characteristics, atmosphere, and environment. 🌞 The Sun The Sun is the star at the centre of our solar system. Its gravity holds the solar system together, while its energy provides light and heat. 🌍 Our Home — Earth Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only world currently known to support life. 🪐 The Largest Planet Jupiter is the largest planet in our solar system, with a diameter approximately 11 times that of Earth. 🔥 The Hottest Planet Venus is the hottest planet in our solar system because its thick atmosphere traps heat through an intense greenhouse effect. 💍 The Ringed Planet Saturn is famous for its spectacular rings, which are made mostly of countless particles of ice and rocky material. 🔴 The Red Planet Mars appears reddish because iron minerals in its soil have oxidised, creating a rusty colour. 🌌 The Distant Planet Neptune is the most distant of the eight major planets from the Sun. ☄️ Beyond the Planets Our solar system also contains dwarf planets, moons, asteroids, comets, and countless smaller objects waiting to be explored.
